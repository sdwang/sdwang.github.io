About

Si Wang is a science fiction and fantasy writer, software engineer, and former neuroscience researcher. His short stories have appeared in PodCastle and Aurealis and often explore themes of identity, memory, and human connection. He lives with his family in the San Francisco Bay Area. You can find him on Twitter (@siwang) and Bluesky (@si-wang.bsky.social).
